How to use it

Match the clip to the line

Play each clip while the host says the line on its card.

Bring your own sound

Clips have no audio. The host’s voice carries the read.

Trim, reorder, add graphics

Cut clips to fit your pacing and lay your lower thirds on top.

End on the end card

Close on the Cloaked end card. Add the partner URL and offer as your own graphic.

Do and don’t

Do

  • Match each clip to what the host is saying.
  • Trim and reorder clips to fit the read.
  • Add your own lower thirds, captions and music.
  • Show the partner URL and offer as your own graphic.

Don’t

  • Add text saying Cloaked removes 100% of your information.
  • Say Cloaked makes you anonymous or impossible to track.
  • Show uncensored personal information on screen.
  • Suggest every scan finds a Social Security number.
  • Recolor, crop, rotate or stretch the Cloaked logo.
